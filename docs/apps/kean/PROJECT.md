# Kean Project

## Purpose

Kean is a Japanese entry portal for understanding UFO and UAP disclosure through basic concepts, modern history, and the people involved.

## Audience

- Japanese readers who discovered UFO/UAP disclosure through recent reporting, hearings, public documents, or the film *Disclosure Day*.
- Readers who want a source-aware introduction that separates confirmed facts, claims, and unverified points.

## MVP Scope

- `/kean` acts as the portal home with a reading-guide-first entry.
- `/kean/about` explains the basic terms and context.
- `/kean/history` presents the modern disclosure timeline.
- `/kean/uap` presents a small index of key UAP videos and object cases.
- `/kean/uap/[id]` provides source-aware UAP explanation pages for Tic Tac, Gimbal, and GoFast.
- `/kean/people` organizes the main figures by category.
- `/kean/people/[id]` provides source-aware individual person pages.
- People pages and modal views use Kean-generated illustration portraits as the canonical on-site image.
- The Tic Tac page uses a local generated/illustrative GLB model as an interactive explanatory asset, not as evidence.

## Out Of Scope

- Individual timeline event pages.
- Search, login, favorites, admin tools, or automated name extraction.
- Making legacy photo assets the primary on-site image source.
