# Keyhoe Design

Keyhoe should feel like a compact daily briefing, not a generic news portal.

## UI Direction

- Prioritize quick scanning in Japanese.
- Keep the tone calm, source-aware, and practical.
- Separate official sources, news, and online discussion clearly.

## Screens

- `/keyhoe`: daily feed, update time, summary, category filter, article cards, feedback/share area.
- `/keyhoe/about`: product explanation and reading guidance.

## Interaction

- Category switching is a filter for one feed, not separate page sections.
- Show the first 12 items by default.
- If more items exist, reveal more with `もっと見る` up to the feed limit.

Detailed legacy spec: `docs/apps/keyhoe-mockup-spec.md`.
